First-bar-of-day drift, and "hold the overnight position to the first bar's close" as an E exit extension (2026-11-04)

Trigger: the 5-min scalp battery showed the 09:30-10:00 bucket drifting up ~+1.5-1.9 bp per 5-min bar on
QQQ/SPY/IWM in Jun-2025 -> Jun-2026 (t ~2). Checked on the longer files:

By year the first-bar drift flips sign often (SPY 60m: 2015 -1.8, 2019 +2.2, 2020 +4.5, 2022 -0.6, 2024 -1.1,
2025 +1.4). The 2025-26 number in the 5-min files is the top of that range, not the norm.

Verdict: the first-bar drift is +1 bp/day with t ~1 over 6-11 years - not an edge on its own. As an E exit
extension (sell at 10:00/10:30 instead of 09:30) it helps SPY/QQQ a little and hurts IWM; mixed by year
(worse in 2024 everywhere). Not adopted. Re-check when there is more data; the audited rule stays 09:30.
